### `[YELLOW]` — Important (Address Within 30 Days)

| # | Risk | Why It Matters | Action |
|---|------|---------------|--------|
| 4 | **Insurance not converted** | A standard homeowner's policy will NOT cover a tenant-occupied property. If a claim occurs while rented under a homeowner policy, the claim will likely be denied. | Get landlord/rental dwelling policy quote before placing a tenant. |
| 5 | **HOA rental restrictions unknown** | Some HOAs prohibit or limit rentals (minimum lease term, approval process, rental caps, tenant registration). If the HOA restricts rentals, your plan may not be viable. | Request CC&Rs and rental rules from HOA. |
| 6 | **Homestead exemption removal impact unknown** | Florida's Save Our Homes caps assessed value increases at 3%/year. Removing homestead resets the assessed value to market value, potentially increasing taxes by $1,000–$4,000+/year. This directly affects cash flow. | Contact Santa Rosa County Property Appraiser to estimate tax increase. |
| 7 | **No property manager selected** | If you PCS, you need a PM in place before you leave. Rushing this decision leads to bad contracts and poor performance. | Begin interviewing PMs now (see Module 10). |
| 8 | **Hurricane / windstorm exposure** | Navarre is directly exposed to Gulf hurricanes. Inadequate wind/flood coverage, high deductibles, or policy gaps can result in catastrophic uninsured losses. | Review all coverage limits, deductibles (especially hurricane %), and exclusions. |
| 9 | **Reserve fund not established** | Without a cash reserve, one major repair or two months of vacancy could force you into financial stress or debt. | Target: $8,000–$15,000 in a dedicated reserve account before placing a tenant. |
| 10 | **Negative cash flow risk** | Florida's rising insurance costs, higher property taxes without homestead, and management fees can easily push a property into negative cash flow even at market rent. | Must run full cash flow analysis (Module 7) with real numbers. |
| 11 | **Roof age and condition unknown** | FL insurers increasingly refuse to insure homes with roofs >15–20 years old. Roof condition also affects wind mitigation credits and insurance pricing. | Determine roof age and get inspection if >10 years old. |
| 12 | **No CPA consulted on rental conversion** | Depreciation basis, land/building split, homestead removal timing, and Schedule E setup require professional guidance. Errors can cost thousands in missed deductions or IRS issues. | Consult CPA before or at the time of rental conversion. |
