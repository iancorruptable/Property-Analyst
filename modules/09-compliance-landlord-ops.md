### Florida Notice Periods

| Action | Notice Required | Statute |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| Non-renewal (annual lease) | 60 days before end (check lease terms) | Lease / §83.57 |
| Termination (month-to-month) | 15 days before end of monthly period | §83.57 |
| Rent increase | At least 30 days (or per lease terms) | Best practice |
| 3-Day Notice (nonpayment of rent) | 3 business days | §83.56(3) |
| 7-Day Notice (lease violation, curable) | 7 days to cure | §83.56(2)(b) |
| 7-Day Notice (lease violation, non-curable) | 7 days, no cure | §83.56(2)(a) |
| Landlord access / entry | 12 hours reasonable notice | §83.53 |
