## 30-Day PCS Prep Checklist

> Start this checklist 30 days before your departure date.

### Week 1: Decisions and Planning (Days 30–24)

- [ ] **Decide: Keep as rental or sell**
  - Run decision engine (Module 17)
  - Review cash flow projections (Module 7)
  - Assess VA entitlement impact (Module 3)
- [ ] **Select property manager** (if renting)
  - Interview 2–3 companies
  - Sign management agreement
  - See Module 10 for questions and red flags
- [ ] **Consult CPA**
  - Discuss rental conversion tax implications
  - Establish depreciation basis
  - Plan homestead exemption removal
- [ ] **Contact insurance agent**
  - Begin conversion from homeowner to landlord policy
  - Verify flood and wind coverage continues
  - Get umbrella policy if not already in place
- [ ] **Review HOA rental rules**
  - Request CC&Rs if not already obtained
  - Submit rental approval application if required
  - Register with HOA management company
- [ ] **Prepare Power of Attorney** (if needed)
  - Special POA for property management decisions
  - May need notarization / consular services if OCONUS
- [ ] **Set up reserve fund**
  - Target: 3–6 months of total property expenses
  - Include mortgage, taxes, insurance, HOA, maintenance buffer
  - Fund a separate account

### Week 2: Property Preparation (Days 23–17)

- [ ] **Property inspection and repairs**
  - Walk through entire property
  - Document condition (photos + video)
  - Complete necessary repairs (see Module 6)
  - Address deferred maintenance items
- [ ] **HVAC service** — tune-up, filter replacement
- [ ] **Pest control treatment**
- [ ] **Landscaping** — trim trees, mow, edge
- [ ] **Clean property** — deep clean all areas
- [ ] **Paint / patch** — touch up or repaint as needed
- [ ] **Rekey locks** — new locks or rekey before tenant
- [ ] **Smoke / CO detectors** — test, replace batteries
- [ ] **Appliance check** — verify all working
- [ ] **Roof check** — visual inspection
- [ ] **Plumbing check** — look for leaks, test all fixtures

### Week 3: Documentation and Handoff (Days 16–10)

- [ ] **Create digital document vault** — scan and upload all:
  - Closing disclosure and deed
  - Mortgage statements
  - Insurance policies (HO, flood, wind, umbrella)
  - HOA documents and CC&Rs
  - Property tax records
  - VA COE
  - Utility account info
  - Vendor contact list
  - Home warranty (if any)
  - Appliance manuals / warranties
- [ ] **Complete video walkthrough** — record entire property, narrate condition
- [ ] **Prepare lease** (if tenant already identified) or authorize PM to list
- [ ] **Set up rental listing** (with PM or independently)
  - Professional photos
  - Listing description
  - Pricing based on comps (Module 6)
- [ ] **Prepare required FL disclosures** (Module 9)
- [ ] **Set up owner portal** with PM (online access to statements, maintenance)
- [ ] **Establish communication cadence** with PM (weekly during vacancy, monthly when occupied)
- [ ] **Set up separate bank account** for rental income/expenses
- [ ] **Establish mail forwarding** — USPS + update addresses on all accounts
- [ ] **Give PM spare keys, garage remotes, gate codes**
- [ ] **Provide PM emergency vendor list** (or approve their vendors)

### Week 4: Final Actions (Days 9–1)

- [ ] **Transfer or continue utilities**
  - If tenant: verify transfer plan
  - If vacant: keep on in owner name (water to prevent pipe issues, electric for showings)
- [ ] **Confirm insurance conversion effective date**
- [ ] **Confirm homestead exemption removal timeline** with county
- [ ] **Verify PM has all access** — keys, codes, lockbox, gate
- [ ] **Verify PM has all documents** — lease template, disclosures, inspection forms
- [ ] **Final property walkthrough** — last check before departure
- [ ] **Take final photos** — timestamped, all rooms
- [ ] **Confirm emergency local contact** — someone who can access property on short notice
- [ ] **Set up automatic mortgage payment** (if not already)
- [ ] **Set calendar reminders** for:
  - Monthly PM review
  - Insurance renewal date
  - Tax due dates
  - Lease expiration
  - HOA dues

## Contingency Plans

### If Tenant Placement is Delayed

| Scenario | Action | Financial Impact |
|----------|--------|-----------------|
| Vacant 1 month | PM continues marketing; reduce price 3–5% if no interest | 1 month lost rent + utilities |
| Vacant 2 months | Review pricing aggressively; consider concessions (first month discount) | 2 months lost rent + utilities |
| Vacant 3+ months | Reassess market rent; consider short-term rental; evaluate selling | 3+ months lost rent + mortgage stress |

**Vacancy Reserve Target:** _[3 months mortgage + expenses = $______]_

### If House Sits Vacant Long-Term

- Ensure utilities remain on (prevent pipe damage, mold)
- PM inspects at least every 2 weeks
- Verify insurance vacancy clause (most policies void after 30–60 days vacant)
- Consider house sitter or short-term arrangement
- Adjust insurance if needed (vacant dwelling policy)
- Winterize if applicable (N/A for Navarre FL, but monitor for tropical storms)

### Reoccupancy Plan (If I Return)

| Step | Action | Timeline |
|------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Provide notice to PM of intent to reoccupy | 90+ days before |
| 2 | Verify lease end date — do not violate tenant's lease rights | Check lease |
| 3 | If tenant month-to-month, provide proper notice (15 days FL) | Per statute |
| 4 | If tenant fixed-term, wait for lease expiration or negotiate | Cannot force out mid-lease |
| 5 | Re-apply for homestead exemption | By March 1 of reoccupancy year |
| 6 | Convert insurance back to homeowner policy | Before move-in |
| 7 | Cancel PM agreement per contract terms | Per cancellation clause |
| 8 | Inspect property condition | Before or at move-in |
| 9 | Document any tenant-caused damage | For deposit reconciliation |
