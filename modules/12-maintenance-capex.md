### Severity Guide
- **Emergency** — Immediate risk to safety or property (water main break, gas leak, fire damage)
- **Urgent** — Significant habitability issue (AC failure in summer, no hot water, major leak)
- **Moderate** — Functional issue, not emergency (appliance malfunction, minor leak, broken fixture)
- **Low** — Cosmetic or convenience (paint touch-up, squeaky door, weatherstripping)

### Fix Type
- **Temporary** — Stopgap until permanent repair
- **Permanent** — Issue fully resolved
- **Deferred** — Acknowledged but not yet addressed (document reason)
