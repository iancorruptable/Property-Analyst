## Insurance Conversion Checklist (Homeowner → Landlord)

> Must be completed before tenant occupancy

- [ ] Contact current insurer about conversion to landlord/rental dwelling policy
- [ ] If insurer doesn't offer landlord policy, get quotes from competitors
- [ ] Verify dwelling coverage is at replacement cost
- [ ] Add loss-of-rent coverage (Coverage D equivalent)
- [ ] Verify liability limits are adequate ($300K+ recommended)
- [ ] Check vacancy clause — how long can property be vacant before coverage lapses?
- [ ] Add property manager as additional insured (if required)
- [ ] Verify flood insurance remains in force
- [ ] Verify windstorm coverage remains in force
- [ ] Get umbrella policy or increase umbrella to cover rental liability
- [ ] Confirm no exclusions for tenant-occupied property
- [ ] Document all policy numbers and renewal dates in Module 2
