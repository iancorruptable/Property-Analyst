## Questions to Ask Before Hiring a Property Manager

### Company Background
- [ ] How long have you been managing properties in the Navarre / Santa Rosa County area?
- [ ] How many units do you currently manage?
- [ ] Are you a licensed FL real estate broker? (Required for PM in FL)
- [ ] Do you carry errors & omissions and general liability insurance?
- [ ] Can you provide 3 owner references?

### Fees and Financial
- [ ] What is your monthly management fee percentage?
- [ ] What is your leasing fee for a new tenant?
- [ ] What is your renewal fee?
- [ ] Do you mark up maintenance/repair costs? By how much?
- [ ] Are there any other fees (setup, inspection, advertising, technology, eviction)?
- [ ] What is the minimum owner reserve you require?
- [ ] How and when are owner disbursements made?
- [ ] Do you provide monthly owner statements? What format?
- [ ] What accounting software do you use?

### Tenant and Leasing
- [ ] What is your tenant screening process? (Credit, background, employment, rental history)
- [ ] What are your minimum tenant qualification criteria?
- [ ] What is your average time to fill a vacancy?
- [ ] What lease template do you use? Can I review it?
- [ ] How do you handle lease renewals? What notice do you give?
- [ ] How do you handle lease violations?
- [ ] What is your eviction process and who pays attorney fees?

### Maintenance and Operations
- [ ] What is the owner approval threshold for repairs?
- [ ] Do you use in-house maintenance or outside vendors?
- [ ] How do you handle after-hours emergencies?
- [ ] How often do you inspect the property?
- [ ] What does your inspection report look like? (Request sample)
- [ ] How do you handle hurricane preparation for properties?

### Communication and Technology
- [ ] Do you have an owner portal? Tenant portal?
- [ ] How quickly do you respond to owner inquiries?
- [ ] How do you communicate maintenance issues or tenant concerns?
- [ ] Do you provide year-end tax reporting (1099, expense summaries)?

### Contract Terms
- [ ] What is the contract length?
- [ ] What are the cancellation terms and penalties?
- [ ] Does the contract auto-renew?
- [ ] Who owns the tenant relationship if I cancel? (Can I keep the tenant?)

## Red Flags in Management Agreements

| Red Flag | Why It Matters | Check |
|----------|---------------|-------|
| Excessive cancellation penalty | Locks you in if performance is poor | [ ] |
| Markup on maintenance not disclosed | Hidden costs inflate expenses | [ ] |
| No owner approval threshold (PM spends freely) | Could authorize expensive repairs without consent | [ ] |
| Fee on owner-performed work | Some PMs charge even when you handle repairs | [ ] |
| Automatic rent increase clauses that benefit PM | Incentivizes PM to raise rent (higher % fee) vs. tenant retention | [ ] |
| No inspection included in fee | Should inspect at least 2x/year | [ ] |
| PM retains late fees entirely | Common — but negotiate to split or keep | [ ] |
| Long auto-renewal with short opt-out window | Easy to miss window and get locked in another year | [ ] |
| PM claims ownership of tenant lease upon cancellation | You lose the tenant and have to re-lease | [ ] |
| No E&O insurance | You bear liability for PM's mistakes | [ ] |
| No owner portal / no monthly statements | Cannot monitor remotely | [ ] |

## Monthly Owner Review Checklist

Use this checklist every month to monitor your PM:

- [ ] **Statement received?** — Owner statement received by 15th of following month
- [ ] **Rent collected on time?** — Was rent collected by the due date?
- [ ] **Correct rent amount?** — Does the statement show the right rent?
- [ ] **Management fee correct?** — Fee matches contract percentage
- [ ] **Maintenance charges reasonable?** — Review each charge; request invoices if needed
- [ ] **Reserve balance adequate?** — PM should maintain minimum reserve
- [ ] **Vacancy status** — If vacant, what marketing is being done?
- [ ] **Tenant issues?** — Any complaints, violations, or delinquencies?
- [ ] **Inspections current?** — When was the last inspection?
- [ ] **Insurance and taxes current?** — Confirm payments from escrow or PM
- [ ] **Communication satisfactory?** — PM responding within 24 hours?
- [ ] **Year-to-date financials tracking?** — Compare to budget
