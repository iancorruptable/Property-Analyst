## VA Entitlement Calculation Framework

### How VA Entitlement Works (Reference)

```
Basic Entitlement:             $36,000
Bonus (Tier 2) Entitlement:    Varies — based on county loan limit

2026 County Loan Limit (Standard): $766,550 (confirm for current year)

Maximum Guaranty = 25% of county loan limit
Standard Max Guaranty = $766,550 × 25% = $191,637.50

Entitlement Charged on Current Loan = 25% of original loan amount
                                      (capped at max guaranty for that county at time of origination)

Remaining Entitlement = Max Guaranty − Entitlement Charged on Active Loan(s)

Zero-Down Buying Power = Remaining Entitlement × 4
                         (because VA guarantees 25%, so entitlement × 4 = max loan with no down payment)

If zero-down buying power < desired purchase price:
  Down Payment = 25% of the amount ABOVE zero-down buying power
```
