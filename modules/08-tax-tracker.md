### Key Tax Flags

- `[YELLOW]` **Depreciation recapture:** When you sell, ALL depreciation claimed (or that should have been claimed) is recaptured at up to 25% federal tax rate
- `[YELLOW]` **Section 121 exclusion:** If you lived in the home 2 of the last 5 years before sale, you may exclude up to $250K ($500K married) of gain — but not depreciation recapture
- `[YELLOW]` **1031 Exchange:** Could defer gain by exchanging into another investment property — must follow strict timelines
- `[YELLOW]` **Military exception:** Extended duty may extend the 5-year lookback period for Section 121
