## Appreciation Formulas

```
Total Appreciation ($)     = Current Value − Purchase Price
Total Appreciation (%)     = (Current Value − Purchase Price) ÷ Purchase Price × 100
Annualized Appreciation    = ((Current Value ÷ Purchase Price) ^ (1 ÷ Years Owned)) − 1) × 100
Years Owned                = (Today − Purchase Date) ÷ 365.25
```
